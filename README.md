One image summing up Friday 27 November 2026's world news: UK net migration at its lowest since 1997, Israel kills 10 in Gaza as Hamas weighs its reply, a tanker capsizes off Ibiza, Gukesh and Sindarov draw marathon Game 3 of the World Chess Championship, a Thanksgiving storm leaves 500,000+ without power in the US, and Ebola in Congo passes 12,000 cases. Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261127).

`render.py` draws the image with Pillow (DejaVu Sans); `main.ts` copies the rendered `entry.png` to the output directory and verifies it byte for byte.
